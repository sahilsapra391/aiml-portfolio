Artifact 05 · Workshop 5 · Feedback & Iteration

Iterative Design & Feedback Integration

When peer feedback fell through, I turned to an AI partner to simulate a design review. This artifact is the changelog of how I treated this portfolio as a living product and improved it.

Continuous Iteration UX Design AI Design Review Product Mindset
Field 01 — Introduction

What this artifact is

Artifact 5 was designed to showcase the incorporation of peer feedback to improve previous work. However, when the deadline approached and no peer feedback had materialized on my Workshop 4 submission, I had a choice: submit an empty placeholder or find another way to run a review cycle.

I chose the latter. I attached my entire portfolio codebase to an LLM, prompted it to act as an objective product design reviewer, and executed a simulated design review. This page documents the three targeted enhancements that came out of that session, which are now live across the site.

Field 02 — Description

The three enhancements

Based on the AI design review, I implemented three structural and content improvements across Artifacts 1 through 4:

  • Enhancement 1: Cross-Navigation (UX)
    The original site relied on a hub-and-spoke model where users had to return to the homepage to view the next artifact. I implemented persistent "Next" and "Previous" buttons at the bottom of every page to create a seamless, linear narrative for a hiring manager navigating my work.
  • Enhancement 2: Elevating Business Impact
    While my artifacts included a "Value Proposition," the AI review pointed out they could lean harder into B2B software alignment. I added a targeted "PM Takeaway" to the Value Proposition of every artifact, explicitly translating the technical work into operational business metrics (e.g., compute costs, ROI, false-positive risk).
  • Enhancement 3: Ecosystem Cross-Linking
    To make the portfolio feel like a cohesive ecosystem rather than isolated assignments, I added cross-links within the "Relevance" fields. For example, Artifact 2 (Model Selection) now explicitly links to Artifact 4 (Drift), noting that even perfectly selected models will decay in production.
Field 03 — Objective

Why I did it

The objective was to satisfy the rubric's "Feedback Integration" requirement while demonstrating a core product management trait: bias for action. Waiting on a blocked dependency (peer feedback) isn't an option when trying to ship. Pivoting to an AI solution allowed me to critically evaluate my own work, prioritize high-value updates, and ship the final increment on time.

Field 04 — Value Proposition

Why this artifact matters

Shipping V1 is the baseline. This artifact proves that I don't just ship and walk away; I actively seek feedback, identify friction points in the user experience, and push iterative updates that drive business value. It shows that I treat everything I build — even a graduate portfolio — as a living product.
